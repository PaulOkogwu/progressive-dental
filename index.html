Welcome to Progressive Dental Studio Inc.

Since 1999, Progressive Dental Studio has provided consistently high quality, personal service to the professional dental industry.

Our products and services have a well-known reputation for customer focus and attention to detail. Our dedicated team of highly trained technicians is focused on meeting your requirements.

Your patients’ needs are our top priority: a quality finished product, delivered efficiently and on time.
